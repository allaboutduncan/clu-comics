If “Current server time” does not match the clock you are reading this on, your offset is wrong and every schedule will fire that far out. The offset is fixed and does not follow daylight saving, so a zone that observes it needs a different value in summer than in winter — US Central, for example, is UTC-05:00 from March to November and UTC-06:00 the rest of the year.
