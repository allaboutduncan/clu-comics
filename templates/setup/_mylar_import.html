Import from Mylar3

The importer reads a copy of Mylar's mylar.db and config.ini. Your watchlist becomes Pull List series, and your SABnzbd or NZBGet client, Newznab indexers and ComicVine key come across too. You review everything before anything changes.

Not imported: Torznab indexers, torrent clients and blackhole folders. Mylar's own database is never opened; the files are copied, read and deleted.

Before you start
  • A library that holds your Mylar series folders
  • Metron, to identify each series. Without it, series are added with ComicVine-only ids.
