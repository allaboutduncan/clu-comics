Metadata

Metadata providers tell CLU what each comic is (series, issue, credits, cover date) and supply the release lists behind the pull list and Wanted. Credentials are tested before they are saved.

Metron is required for the pull list, Wanted, subscriptions, the folder scan and the Mylar3 import. ComicVine is optional.
