Users and sharing

Until an account has a password, CLU needs no login: whoever opens it is the owner. Set an owner password and everyone has to log in. Add more accounts and each person gets their own reading progress, lists and permissions.

Will anyone else use this CLU?
Without an owner password CLU is unauthenticated: anyone who can reach this address can use and change everything, and reading apps connect over OPDS with no password. Keep it on your home network or behind a reverse proxy with its own login, or set an owner password below to turn login on.
{# Shown for either answer while the owner has no password: a password turns login on even for one person. #}
