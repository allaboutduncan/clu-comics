Users and sharing

With one account, CLU needs no login: whoever opens it is the owner. Add a second account and everyone has to log in, and each person gets their own reading progress, lists and permissions.

Will anyone else use this CLU?
CLU stays login-free. Anyone who can reach this address can use and change everything, so keep it on your home network or behind a reverse proxy with its own login. Reading apps connect over OPDS with no password.
